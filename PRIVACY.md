# Privacy Policy — RealTime AI Cam (Android & iOS)

**Effective date: November 4, 2026**

RealTime AI Cam is built by Nice Dreamz LLC ("we", "us"). This policy covers the RealTime AI Cam mobile app on Android and iOS.

## The short version

**RealTime AI Cam does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Period.**

Everything the app does — object detection, text recognition (OCR), translation, describing photos, and Live Cam conversations — happens entirely on your device. Nothing you point your camera at ever leaves your phone.

## Details

- **Camera**: The app uses your camera to run live AI detection, read text, and translate. Camera frames are processed in real time on your device and are never recorded, saved, or transmitted anywhere.
- **No accounts**: The app has no sign-up, no login, and no user profiles.
- **No data leaves your phone**: The AI models (object detection, OCR, translation, and the built-in What's this? model) are bundled with the app and run offline. Since 1.4.0 the app can also download larger What's this? models once from our own server (ineedhemp.com), automatically on Wi-Fi or when you tap download; that request only fetches the model files and carries no photos, text, location, or account. After that they run offline too. The app does not send your images, text, location, or any other data to us or to any third party.
- **No analytics or ads**: The app contains no advertising SDKs, no trackers, and no analytics libraries.
- **No data sale**: We have nothing to sell, because we collect nothing.

## Permissions

- **Camera** — required for the app's core features (live detection, text reading, translation). Used only while the app is open.
- **Microphone and speech recognition** — used only to hear questions you ask out loud: while you hold the ask button in What's this?, while you speak to Help Me Aim, and the whole time Live Cam is open. Your words are turned into text by Apple's speech recognition on your phone (on-device only, it works in airplane mode). No audio is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere, and Live Cam's conversation is forgotten when you leave it.
- **Photos (add only)** — Help Me Aim saves the pictures you take to your photo library. Nothing is uploaded.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and note the new effective date above.
